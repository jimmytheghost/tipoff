Tipoff Privacy

Tipoff’s schedule is public. If you save settings while signed out, Tipoff stores your selected time zone and teams in this browser.

If you sign in with Google, Tipoff verifies your Google sign-in and uses your Google account identifier to save your settings privately on the server. Tipoff does not store your Google password, email address, or access tokens. Tipoff uses a cookie to keep this browser signed in for up to 30 days.

Signing out ends the session on this browser. To clear settings saved while signed out, clear site data in your browser. To request deletion of account settings and sessions, contact jimmytheghost@gmail.com.
